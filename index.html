OUR STORY

About NepaliSafari.

A simpler way to share the road and discover Nepal together.

NS
MADE FOR THE JOURNEY

Connecting people, one shared ride at a time.

NepaliSafari helps passengers find seats on journeys vehicle owners are already making. It brings routes, clear per-seat prices, and trip details together so people can plan a more convenient journey across Nepal.

How NepaliSafari worksFrom finding a route to completing a trip
01

Find a journey

Passengers enter their departure, destination, and travel date to browse available shared rides and compare the route, seats, and fare.

02

Owners offer seats

Vehicle owners post their route, departure time, open seats, vehicle information, and an estimated fare based on distance and shared travel costs.

03

Request a seat

Passengers review and accept the booking terms before sending a seat request. The vehicle owner can accept or decline it in My trips.

04

Chat and travel

Once the owner accepts, passenger and owner can chat to coordinate. Both travellers confirm trip completion in their accounts.

SAFETY AND TRANSPARENCY

Clear details. Safer journeys.

Accounts, rides, bookings, messages, complaints, and reviews are stored in the local server database. Profile and driving-licence images are encrypted before storage. Administrators can review complete owner profiles; this is an internal review, not an official government identity check.

Local installation: This app currently runs only on this computer. Password sign-in is active. Online payments, public hosting, HTTPS, account recovery, and independent identity verification still need to be configured before the service is opened to the public. Do not expose the local server to the internet.
